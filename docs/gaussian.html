About the Previous Stable Recipe

The recipe from May 15 uses 1,800 axis-aligned Gaussians, occupied-voxel initialization, initial scales of about 1.59 mm, and weighted full-volume fitting with foreground and uniform samples. This reproduction uses the historical subject ordering and records patient identity in its checkpoints.

The original 0.9503 IoU was a sampled-point diagnostic. The reproduced sample score and native-grid scores are reported separately. The refined model receives three times as many optimizer steps; both versions use the same model size, full-label supervision, and seed.

ΔK and mesh component counts remain visible because a high overlap score can coexist with holes or fragments. Matching integrated curvature is not evidence of matching anatomy. These are two reference examples, not population-level validation results.
